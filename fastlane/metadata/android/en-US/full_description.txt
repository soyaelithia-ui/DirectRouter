DirectRouter is an open-source, no-root Android app that turns your phone into a Wi-Fi Direct Router or a local proxy server over Native Tethering, with a built-in SOCKS5 VPN Client mode for full traffic tunneling on a second Android device.

Sharing modes:
- Wi-Fi Direct Router: creates a local Wi-Fi Direct group with an instant Wi-Fi QR code, configurable SSID/passphrase and 2.4/5 GHz band (Android 10+), plus Auto-Proxy PAC and manual proxy details.
- Native Tethering Proxy: shares internet over your phone's native hotspot, USB tethering, or LAN without touching Wi-Fi Direct or installing any app on client devices.
- VPN Client Mode (2nd Android phone): uses hev-socks5-tunnel with mapdns, IPv6 support, and MTU 1500 to tunnel all device traffic (TCP, UDP, DNS, and IPv6) through the host SOCKS5 proxy.

Multi-protocol single-port proxy (default port 8080):
- HTTP and HTTPS (CONNECT)
- WebSocket (ws:// and wss://)
- Auto-Proxy PAC (/proxy.pac)
- SOCKS4 / SOCKS4a
- SOCKS5 (TCP and UDP Associate)

Security and customization:
- Safe local interface binding by default (Wi-Fi Direct or active tethering interface only) with optional specific-IP selection and random port generator.
- Anti-SSRF protection for TCP and UDP blocking loopback, link-local, and self-proxy targets by default.
- Optional proxy authentication (HTTP Basic and SOCKS5 RFC 1929) with constant-time credential verification and client VPN credential support.
- Configurable concurrent connection limit and sanitized PAC output.
- Light, Dark, AMOLED Black, and Material You (Android 12+) themes.
- Granular notifications with live speed indicator, privacy mode (hides SSID/IP on lock screen), quick stop action, and client/error alerts.
- Built-in OEM compatibility diagnostics for Xiaomi/HyperOS, Samsung OneUI, Huawei/Honor, and Oppo/Vivo.

No root required, no trackers, no telemetry, MIT licensed.
